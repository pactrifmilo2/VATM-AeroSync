## Fix UI khong mo duoc

### Muc tieu

Sua loi WinUI 3 UI tu dong dong ngay khi mo `AeroSync.UI.exe`.

### File/vi tri da sua

- `aerosync-ui/MainWindow.xaml`: bo thuoc tinh `Title` tren root `Window`; tieu de cua WinUI 3 window da duoc dat bang `AppWindow.Title` trong `MainWindow.xaml.cs`.

### Hanh vi truoc va sau

- Truoc: `MainWindow.InitializeComponent()` nem `Microsoft.UI.Xaml.Markup.XamlParseException`, UI khong hien cua so.
- Sau: XAML root khong con gan thuoc tinh khong tuong thich tren `Window`; viec dat tieu de van do code-behind quan ly.

### Kiem tra

- Publish UI sach voi `dotnet publish` thanh cong.
- Chay EXE publish sach truoc khi sua: tai hien loi XAML parse.
- Chay lai EXE sau khi sua: process UI song sau 5 giay trong publish sach.

### Gioi han

- Da build lai release ZIP day du bang scripts/release.ps1 khong dung tuy chon bo qua build.
