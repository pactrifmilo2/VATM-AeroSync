VATM AEROSYNC - CAI DAT / CAP NHAT

Nguoi dung chi can lam 3 buoc:

1. Giai nen toan bo file ZIP.
2. Bam dup vao AeroSync-Deploy.cmd.
3. Chon Yes khi Windows hoi quyen Administrator, sau do cho den khi hien:
   "AeroSync da san sang."

Sau khi cai dat thanh cong, giao dien AeroSync tu dong mo. Cac lan sau,
mo shortcut AeroSync tren Desktop hoac Start Menu.

Khong xoa hoac sua cac file ben trong goi cap nhat.
Khong can go bo phien ban cu. File deploy tu dong giu cau hinh, sao luu va cap nhat.

Lenh danh cho ky thuat khi can:

  AeroSync-Deploy.cmd status
  AeroSync-Deploy.cmd restart
  AeroSync-Deploy.cmd rollback

Neu co loi, gui anh man hinh va file C:\AeroSync\logs\deploy-*.log cho ky thuat.
