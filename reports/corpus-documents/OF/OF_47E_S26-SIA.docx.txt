Explanatory Notes:
(1) Flight number: ICAO code.
(2) Effective from: Start date, in English, format DDMmmYY.
(3) Effective to: End date, in English, format DDMmmYY.
(4) Days of services: Numbers 1–7 correspond to Monday–Sunday; “–” indicates no operation.
(5) (7) Departure/Arrival airport: 4-letter ICAO code.
(6) (8) Scheduled departure/arrival time: UTC time.
(9) Passenger: Includes passenger flight and passenger with belly cargo.
(10) Aircraft type: ICAO code.
(11) Registration Mark:“As per AOC of [Operator name: ICAO code]” or list specific registration marks.
(12) Maximum seats of aircraft type.
(13) Maximum payload of aircraft type.
(14) Aircraft nationality: in English.
(15) Sector: ICAO code (e.g., WMKK-ZGGG).
(16) Airways: List only airways within Vietnam FIR, including primary and alternative airways (if any).
